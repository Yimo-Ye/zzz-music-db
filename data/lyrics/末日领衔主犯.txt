作词 : 杨武韬
作曲 : 杨武韬
演唱：Mr.mo

In the night sky, my stage unfolds,
Tiptoeing on starlight, the world's mine to behold.
The mysteries will shine within my grasp,
Like the scent of flowers, brushed on my chest.
Night calls to me, I have no fear,
Whispering soft sweet dreams as the gentle winds steer.
By the light.
